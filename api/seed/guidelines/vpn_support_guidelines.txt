1. Connection Failures
Approach to solve:
- Verify user credentials and active subscription.
- Ask which server location and protocol the user selected.
- Request any error codes or messages displayed.
- Suggest switching to a different server or protocol (WireGuard, OpenVPN).
- Advise the user to disable local firewalls or antivirus temporarily.
- Instruct to reboot the device and try reconnecting.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I’m sorry you’re unable to connect. I’ve verified that your subscription is active. Could you please let me know which server location and VPN protocol you are using? If possible, try switching to a different server or protocol like WireGuard. Also, please disable any local firewall or antivirus temporarily and reboot your device. Let me know the result, and I’ll assist further.

2. Frequent Disconnections
Approach to solve:
- Ask how often and when disconnections occur.
- Check network stability with a ping test without VPN.
- Recommend changing VPN protocol or port.
- Ensure the VPN client is updated to the latest version.
- Guide on adjusting MTU or fragmentation settings.
- Collect client logs for engineering review if issue persists.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I apologize for the repeated disconnects. Could you tell me how often and under what conditions you get disconnected? Please run a ping test without the VPN to check your network stability. Meanwhile, try switching the VPN protocol or port, and ensure your client is updated. If it still disconnects, I’ll collect logs for our engineering team.

3. Slow Performance
Approach to solve:
- Verify user’s baseline internet speed without VPN.
- Recommend connecting to a geographically closer server.
- Suggest a protocol optimized for speed.
- Check for local bandwidth usage by other applications.
- Advise pausing large downloads or streaming services.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I’m sorry your connection is slow. Can you share your internet speed test without the VPN? For better performance, please connect to a server closer to your location and use our speed-optimized protocol. Also, check if other applications are consuming bandwidth. Let me know if this improves your speed.

4. Geo-restricted Content Access
Approach to solve:
- Confirm which service or website the user attempts to access.
- Test different country servers that are supported by the service.
- Instruct to clear browser cache and use incognito mode.
- Offer a dedicated or static IP option if available.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I understand you’re having trouble accessing [Service]. Please try connecting to servers in [Country A] or [Country B], which are known to work with that service. Clear your browser cache or use incognito mode and attempt again. We also offer a dedicated IP option if you need consistent access.

5. IP or DNS Leak Concerns
Approach to solve:
- Ask the user to run an online leak test (dnsleaktest.com, ipleak.net).
- Verify that kill-switch and DNS-leak protection are enabled.
- Guide the user to configure secure DNS servers in the client.
- Provide steps to disable IPv6 if leaks persist.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I’m sorry you’re concerned about leaks. Please run a DNS leak test at dnsleaktest.com and share the results. Ensure our kill-switch and DNS leak protection are enabled in settings. If you still see leaks, configure secure DNS servers like 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8 and disable IPv6 in your network settings.

6. Client Installation or Update Errors
Approach to solve:
- Verify operating system version and compatibility.
- Ask for specific error messages or codes during install.
- Walk through a clean uninstall and reinstall process.
- Provide an offline installer or alternative download link.
- Escalate persistent errors to the development team.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I’m sorry you’re experiencing installation errors. Which OS version are you on, and what error message do you see? Let’s try uninstalling the VPN client completely and then reinstalling the latest version. I’ll also send you an offline installer link. If the issue continues, I’ll escalate it to our development team.

7. Multi-Device Connection Limit
Approach to solve:
- Check the number of active device connections on the user’s account.
- Advise disconnecting from unused or forgotten devices.
- Show how to manage and remove devices via the account portal.
- Offer information on plan upgrades for additional connections.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I see you’ve reached the connection limit of [X] devices. Please disconnect any unused devices from your account portal. If you need more simultaneous connections, consider upgrading your plan, and I can share the upgrade options.

8. Billing and Subscription Issues
Approach to solve:
- Confirm payment method and review transaction details.
- Check subscription status, renewal date, and plan type.
- Assist with retrying payment or updating billing information.
- Process cancellations or refunds per policy.
- Escalate complex disputes to the finance department.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

It looks like your renewal payment for plan [Plan Name] did not go through. Please verify your payment information or try another method. If you prefer, I can process the payment manually. For any refund or cancellation questions, I can assist or forward your case to our finance team.

9. Server Outages or Maintenance
Approach to solve:
- Check the service status page for known issues.
- Communicate estimated resolution time to the user.
- Recommend alternative server locations during the outage.
- Monitor updates and inform the user of progress.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

Our status page indicates a scheduled maintenance affecting some servers. We expect resolution by [Time]. Meanwhile, please connect to [Alternative Server Group] for uninterrupted service. I will update you once maintenance is complete.

10. Account Access or Permission Problems
Approach to solve:
- Verify the user’s email address and account status.
- Assist with password resets and account unlock procedures.
- Check for any account suspensions or security holds.
- Escalate unauthorized access or security concerns to the security team.

Sample reply:
Hello [Name],

I’ve reset your password and unlocked your account. Please try logging in now with the new credentials. If you still can’t access your account, let me know, and I will escalate it to our security team.
